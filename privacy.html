Privacy Policy

Shuka Drive is a private tool operated by one individual for personal use. It uses Google OAuth with the single scope drive.file to create and read files in that individual's own Google Drive. It does not access any other files in the account.

Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to store and retrieve the files the tool itself created. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
